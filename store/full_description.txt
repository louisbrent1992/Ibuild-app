iBuild is a phone-first control panel for your mobile app builds. Start a build on any branch, watch it move through Fetch, Test, Build and Upload as it happens, and see when it reaches your testers, without opening a laptop.

Your builds run on Codemagic; iBuild drives them from your phone.

- Start, cancel and rerun builds on any branch and workflow
- Follow every step live, with logs that keep scrolling while they run
- See when an iOS build finishes processing in TestFlight, or when an Android build lands in Google Play as a draft
- Know straight away when a build stopped because of your Codemagic plan, not your code
- Download .ipa and .aab artifacts
- Manage TestFlight tester groups and share public invitation links
- Point iBuild at a GitHub repository and it detects your Flutter project, its platforms and its workflows
- No codemagic.yaml yet? Generate one for iOS and Android, preview it, and commit it straight to your repository
- An optional account keeps your projects in sync across your devices
- Demo mode lets you explore everything before connecting anything

Free includes demo mode and one project. iBuild Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription, adds unlimited projects, committing codemagic.yaml to your repository, and TestFlight public links.

Private by design: your access tokens stay in your device's secure storage and go only to Codemagic, GitHub and Apple. No analytics and no ads.

Requires a Codemagic account. Build minutes and machines come from your Codemagic plan. GitHub and App Store Connect connections are optional.
